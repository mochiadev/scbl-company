A mixed-use civic quarter combining Grade-A office space, ground-floor retail, and a boutique hotel keyed around a landscaped public plaza. Signature Crest led the master-plan, procured the design team, and provided full construction management on behalf of an institutional joint venture.

The scheme has since achieved a stabilised occupancy of 96% and set new headline rents for the sub-market.
