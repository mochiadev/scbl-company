A meticulous restoration of a colonial-era private residence, retaining the original masonry envelope while introducing a contemporary steel-and-glass rear pavilion that opens onto a re-landscaped garden. The heritage fabric was documented, catalogued, and reinstated in lime-based mortars.

Contemporary services were routed through concealed chases so the interior reads as an unbroken continuation of the original plan.
