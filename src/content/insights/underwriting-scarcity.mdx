Prestige and scarcity are not the same thing. A location can be famous, expensive, and still slowly lose ground to a quieter address one district over.

## The three variables

We underwrite on three primary axes.

**1. Constrained supply.** Not just current supply, but the pipeline of consented sites within a fifteen-minute drive.

**2. Migrating demand.** Are the businesses and households moving *into* the catchment older or younger, wealthier or less wealthy, more mobile or more rooted?

**3. Infrastructure delta.** Public investment — power, water, transport — arrives ten years before it prices in. Underwriting only what is already visible on the ground is a slow way to lose.

Great locations compound. Prestigious ones frequently do not.
